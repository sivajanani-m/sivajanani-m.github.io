Survive the School: Quarantine, Book 1: The Loners

A school sealed off by the military. Gangs built out of high school cliques. And one loner trying to keep his little brother alive.

Pretty much this story is about David Thorpe, a loner at McKinley High School. After a huge explosion at the school, David tries to help his English teacher, but the teacher dies right in front of him. A year later, a virus has infected all of the students. It only affects teenagers, makes them lose their hair, and makes them deadly to adults and children. The military has quarantined the school, and the teachers are gone.

Cliques Turned Into Gangs

Without any adults, the students formed gangs based on high school cliques, like the Varsity, the Freaks, and the Pretty Ones. Once a week the government drops food off, and the gangs fight over it. The rule is pretty simple: if you don't have a gang, you're as good as dead.

I really like how the author takes normal school social groups and turns them into something life or death.

David and Will

David doesn't have a gang. It's just him and his younger brother Will, who is epileptic. David has had to act like a parent ever since their mom died and their dad started drinking. This makes every decision harder, because he isn't only trying to survive for himself, he's trying to keep Will safe too.

Final Thoughts

Overall, I really enjoyed this book. It starts fast and it keeps going, so it was easy to keep reading. I thought the plot was stronger than the characters, but the relationship between David and Will was my favorite part.

I'd recommend this book to anyone who likes dystopian stories and fast paced thrillers.

My Rating

★★★★☆

4/5 — a fast, intense read with a great brother story
