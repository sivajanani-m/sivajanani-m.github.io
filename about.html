Hey, I'm Sivajanani.

Who am I?

I'm a 7th grader from Las Vegas, and I write this blog mostly for fun — but also because I figured it'd be pretty cool to have a body of work by the time I'm applying to college. I started it before most people my age would even think about that, which I think says something about me (good things, hopefully).

I speak English and Tamil, and I'm working on Hindi and Norwegian. Yes, Norwegian. No, I don't fully know why. But here we are.

What this blog is

This is my corner of the internet where I write about whatever I'm curious about — book reviews, dinosaur discoveries, aerospace research, soccer analysis, and everything in between. I try to write things that are actually interesting to read, not just school-report style.

I started this for fun, but I also won't pretend the college applications didn't cross my mind. Either way, I'm glad it exists. If you're reading this, hi — I hope you find something worth your time here.
