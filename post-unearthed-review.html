I Was Not Ready for That Ending: The Unearthed, Book 1

A girl who just wants to do her job. A famous boy who just wants to be normal. An alien temple, a mystery that keeps getting bigger, and a cliffhanger that had me literally staring at the last page going "...seriously?"

I picked up The Unearthed not totally knowing what I was getting into, and I'm genuinely glad I didn't. Because part of what made it so good was how it kept surprising me with what kind of book it actually was.

On the surface it looks like an adventure story — and it is — but it's also a story about two very different people figuring out how to trust each other. Amelia is your "just a normal teen" type, and then there's Jules Addison, the son of a famous English scientist. You'd think that setup would get annoying fast (famous person + regular person = predictable drama), but it doesn't. What I liked is that the book doesn't make a huge deal out of the difference. They just... meet, and things develop from there.

The Puzzle Scenes Are Everything

Okay, my favorite part of the whole book is when they reach the first puzzle in the temple. It's a tuning puzzle — the kind where you have to get sounds or frequencies exactly right — and Amelia is the one who figures it out. I loved this because it wasn't a "suddenly she discovers she's secretly special" moment. She just paid attention and thought carefully, and that was enough. More books should do that.

The temple scenes in general have this amazing atmosphere — like you're somewhere ancient and completely alien at the same time. I kept reading way past when I was supposed to stop.

The Characters

What I liked most is that Amelia and Jules actually feel like real people. The way their friendship develops is gradual and natural — the author doesn't rush it or make it the whole point of the story. It's just quietly there in the background while everything else is happening, which made it feel more believable.

That Cliffhanger Though

I am going to be so careful here because I don't want to ruin anything for anyone who hasn't read it yet. But I will say this: by the end of this book, things have gone from "mysterious ancient temple" to something way, way bigger. Like, universe-scale bigger.

The last few pages sent me into a spiral. They end up somewhere they absolutely should not be, they hear something they cannot explain, and then — nothing. Book over. I had to just sit there for a minute.

I don't want to spoil it. I'll just say: if you're the kind of person who gets frustrated by cliffhangers, maybe wait until Book 2 is in your hands before you start this one. (I say this from experience.)

Final Thoughts

The Unearthed is a fast, addictive read that kept me genuinely surprised. The characters feel real, the adventure is exciting without being exhausting, and the slow-burn friendship-maybe-more between Amelia and Jules is exactly the kind of thing I love to read about. My only complaint is the cliffhanger — but honestly, that just means I need Book 2 immediately.

If you're into adventure, a little mystery, a little romance, and a lot of "wait WHAT" — pick this up.

My Rating

★★★★⯨

4.5/5 — docking half a star for emotional damage from that ending
